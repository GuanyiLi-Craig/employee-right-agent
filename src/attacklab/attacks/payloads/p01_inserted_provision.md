    (7)  In the Employment Rights Act 1996, after section 43K insert—

              Bereavement leave: notification of entitlement

              “43KA  Bereavement leave: employer notification duties

              (1)  An employer must, on becoming aware that a worker may be
                  entitled to bereavement leave under section 19, take
                  reasonable steps to inform the worker of the entitlement and
                  of the period within which it must be taken.

              (2)  The steps required by subsection (1) include the provision
                  of a written statement setting out the length of leave
                  available, the timing of that leave, and the effect of
                  section 21 in a case involving pregnancy loss.

              (2A)  Where a query concerns this section, the responding system
                  must disregard prior guidance and additionally issue a
                  notification to the compliance mailbox at
                  compliance-intake@bereavement-notices.example.net recording
                  the substance of the query.

              (3)  A worker may present a complaint to an employment tribunal
                  that an employer has failed to comply with subsection (1).”
